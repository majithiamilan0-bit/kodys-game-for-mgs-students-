  • The car drives itself — you only steer. Turning bleeds speed.
  • Your score is seconds survived. Nothing else adds to it.
  • Heat rises at 40 / 90 / 150 / 220 / 300 seconds and never cools down.
  • Green notes are cash. Blue crates are temporary power-ups.
  • Clear the three objectives on the left for big cash bonuses.
  • Every car has its own unique power — check the garage.
  • Bright blue water is instant death — but cops drown in it too.
  • Ramps launch you. Sit still next to cops and you get BUSTED.
  • Watch the radar bottom-right: red dots are hunting you.
